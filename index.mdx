Whether you are a student practicing new skills or a teacher managing assignments and tracking growth, the platform helps you stay organized, engaged, and supported.

## What you can do

Students and teachers use the platform differently. Here is what each role can accomplish:

- **Students**
  - Join classes with a simple join code.
  - Complete assignments and see due dates in one place.
  - Chat with the AI Tutor for personalized practice and hints.
  - Track your progress and celebrate milestones.
- **Teachers**
  - Create classes and invite students with join codes.
  - Build and assign homework, quizzes, and projects.
  - Review student submissions and give feedback.
  - Monitor class-wide and individual progress over time.
