The AI Tutor is designed to help you understand your work without doing it for you. When you get stuck, the Tutor guides you through the reasoning step by step, offers grade-appropriate hints, and generates practice questions targeted to your weak areas. It is available on every assignment and adapts its tone and difficulty to your grade level.

## What the AI Tutor Does

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Explain concepts" icon="lightbulb">
    Breaks down ideas into clear, age-appropriate language. Whether you need a refresher on photosynthesis or a walkthrough of linear equations, the Tutor explains it in a way that matches your grade level.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Give hints" icon="help-circle">
    Offers incremental clues rather than full answers. This keeps you thinking and problem solving rather than passively receiving solutions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Show examples" icon="file-text">
    Provides worked examples that parallel the problem you are facing. You can see the reasoning process and then apply it to your own work.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Adaptive practice" icon="bar-chart-3">
    Generates extra practice questions that adjust in difficulty based on your performance. If you master a topic, questions get harder. If you struggle, they stay focused on fundamentals.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Identify weaknesses" icon="target">
    Analyzes your mistakes across assignments to spot recurring gaps in understanding. It then recommends focused practice on those specific topics.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Encourage and explain mistakes" icon="smile">
    Offers supportive feedback when answers are wrong. The Tutor explains why a choice was incorrect and what to consider next time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How It Works in Practice

Here is an example of how the AI Tutor helps a student who is struggling with adding fractions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Student asks for help">
    After missing a question about adding 1/2 and 1/4, the student clicks the AI Tutor for assistance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tutor explains the concept">
    The Tutor explains that fractions need a common denominator before they can be added. It uses a simple visual analogy, like slices of pizza, to make the idea concrete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A worked example is shown">
    The Tutor walks through a similar problem step by step: finding the common denominator, converting each fraction, adding the numerators, and simplifying the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice set is generated">
    The Tutor creates a personalized practice set that starts with the original skill and gradually introduces variations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example Practice Set

After working through the fraction explanation, the student receives this adaptive practice set:

| Question | Skill |
| --- | --- |
| 1/2 + 1/4 = ? | Basic addition with different denominators |
| 3/4 - 1/8 = ? | Subtraction with different denominators |
| Which is larger: 2/3 or 3/5? | Comparing fractions |
| 5/6 + 1/3 = ? | Addition requiring simplification |

Each question builds on the last. If the student answers correctly, the next question introduces a slight increase in complexity. If the student answers incorrectly, the Tutor offers another hint and a simpler warm-up question before continuing.

<Tip>
  You can ask the AI Tutor for help at any point during an assignment, even before submitting an answer. It is there to support your learning, not to judge.
</Tip>

<Info>
  The AI Tutor never gives you the final answer on a graded question unless your teacher has explicitly enabled answer reveal. It always prioritizes teaching over telling.
</Info>
