The **Practice For You** section creates custom problem sets based on your individual learning history. The system looks at what you have already done, where you struggled, and how much time you spent on each topic. From that, it assembles practice sessions that focus on your actual needs without overwhelming you with material that is too difficult.

## How Practice Recommendations Are Built

The platform considers several signals when building your personalized practice:

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Previous assignments" icon="clipboard-list">
    Topics and question types from recently completed assignments are used to keep your skills fresh and reinforce classroom instruction.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Incorrect answers" icon="x-circle">
    Questions you answered incorrectly are flagged for review. The system groups related mistakes to identify whether the gap is in a specific skill or a broader concept.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Topics practiced" icon="layers">
    The more you practice a topic, the more the system understands your comfort level. It balances review of familiar material with exposure to new challenges.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Scores and accuracy" icon="percent">
    Your performance trends help the system decide whether to increase difficulty, provide more scaffolding, or revisit fundamentals.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Time spent" icon="clock">
    If you spent significantly longer than expected on a topic, the system may recommend additional practice or suggest a concept review before advancing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Repeated mistakes" icon="alert-circle">
    When the same error pattern appears across multiple assignments, the system prioritizes targeted practice and may suggest a tutorial or worked example before continuing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Difficulty Adjustment

The platform adjusts difficulty gradually so practice remains productive and motivating.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start at your current level">
    Your first recommended questions match the difficulty of your most recent performance on that topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Increase after mastery">
    If you answer several consecutive questions correctly, the difficulty increases in small increments, not large jumps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step back when needed">
    If accuracy drops, the system temporarily returns to simpler variations of the same skill to rebuild confidence and understanding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The goal is steady improvement, not instant difficulty spikes. If practice ever feels too easy or too hard, your teacher can manually adjust the difficulty range for your account.
</Tip>

## Example Practice Sets

Here are two examples of how the system builds practice for different students:

**Student A: Struggling with two-digit multiplication**

| Question |
| --- |
| 12 x 3 |
| 14 x 2 |
| 23 x 4 |
| 31 x 5 |
| 42 x 6 |

The set starts with simpler problems and gradually introduces larger numbers and more regrouping.

**Student B: Solid on vocabulary, weak on context clues**

| Question |
| --- |
| Read a short passage and identify the meaning of "benevolent" using context |
| Match words to their closest synonyms from a passage |
| Identify which sentence provides the best evidence for a word's meaning |
| Compare two passages with different tones using the same vocabulary word |

The set isolates context clue reasoning rather than testing memorized definitions.

<Note>
  Practice sets are not tests. They are designed for learning. You can view hints, ask the AI Tutor for help, and retry questions without penalty unless your teacher has configured the assignment otherwise.
</Note>
