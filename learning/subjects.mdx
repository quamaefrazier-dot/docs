The platform covers the full K-12 curriculum with built-in subjects spanning seven academic areas. When you select your grade level, the system automatically adjusts question difficulty, vocabulary, and expectations to match your instructional stage. Teachers are also free to add custom subjects for specialized or local curriculum requirements.

## Grade-Level Difficulty

The platform adapts every subject to the selected grade level. This means the same topic will be presented differently depending on whether you are in elementary, middle, or high school.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Elementary (K-5)">
    Questions use larger visual elements, simpler vocabulary, and foundational concepts. For example, a math problem might ask you to identify shapes or count with pictures. Feedback is friendly and encouraging.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Middle School (6-8)">
    Content introduces more abstract reasoning, multi-step problems, and deeper reading comprehension. Expect short-answer prompts and introductory analysis in social studies and science.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="High School (9-12)">
    Assignments include complex problem solving, essay writing, evidence-based analysis, and AP-level practice where applicable. The AI Tutor provides more detailed explanations and targeted remediation.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Custom Subjects

If your class needs material outside the built-in list, teachers can create custom subjects directly from the teacher dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Subjects page">
    From your teacher dashboard, navigate to **Subjects** and click **Add Custom Subject**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe">
    Enter a subject name and optional description. This helps students identify it in their assignment list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign and track">
    Once created, the custom subject behaves like any built-in subject. You can assign work, track progress, and view mastery data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Custom subjects are perfect for specialty electives, school-specific courses, or cross-curricular projects.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Grade-level difficulty is still applied to custom subjects if you link them to a standard grade range. This ensures assignments remain age appropriate.
</Note>
