Get from sign-in to your dashboard in minutes. Below is the flow for both students and teachers. Pick the tab that matches your role and follow the steps.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Student">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in with Google">
        Go to the sign-in page and click **Sign in with Google**. Choose the Google account you want to use for school.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create your profile">
        Enter your display name and pick a profile picture if you want one. The platform will save this as your student profile.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select your grade">
        Choose the grade level that matches you (K through 12). This helps the AI Tutor recommend the right practice level and helps teachers organize their rosters.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
        After finishing your profile, you are taken to the **Student Dashboard**. Here you will see any classes you have joined, upcoming assignments, and your progress overview.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Join a class with a code">
        To start receiving assignments, join a class by entering the join code your teacher gave you.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Keep your join code handy. You can also enter it later from the **Join Class** button on your dashboard.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Teacher">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in with Google">
        Go to the sign-in page and click **Sign in with Google**. Use the Google account tied to your school email if you have one.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create your profile">
        Enter your display name and add a profile picture. This becomes your teacher profile across all classes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your school">
        Select or type your school name. This helps organize classes and makes it easier for students and admins to find you.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
        After setup, you are taken to the **Teacher Dashboard**. Here you can see your classes, recent assignments, and student activity summaries.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create your first class">
        Click **Create Class**, give it a name and subject, and the platform will generate a join code. Share that code with your students so they can join.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      You can create as many classes as you need. Each class gets its own join code, assignment list, and progress insights.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
