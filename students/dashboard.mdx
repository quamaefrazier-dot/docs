Your dashboard is the central hub for everything you do here. After signing in with Google, you land on a colorful, friendly page that shows your classes, assignments, and progress at a glance. This guide walks through each section so you can find what you need quickly.

## Greeting and Profile

At the top of your dashboard, you will see a personal greeting with your name and grade level. This helps you confirm you are signed into the right account and sets a friendly tone for your learning session.

## Class Cards

Your enrolled classes appear as colorful cards. Each card shows:

- Class name
- Teacher name
- Subject icon or color
- Quick link to enter the class

## Upcoming Assignments

The assignments section lists what is coming up next. You can see:

- Assignment title and class
- Due date and time remaining
- Point value
- Status (pending, submitted, or missed)

A counter shows how many assignments you have completed versus how many are still pending. This helps you stay on top of your workload.

## Progress Rings by Subject

Circular progress indicators show how you are doing in each subject area. Each ring represents:

- Topics mastered in that subject
- Overall completion percentage
- Visual feedback on your learning journey

Hover over any ring to see the subject name and your current percentage.

## AI Tutor Shortcut

A prominent button or card gives you one-click access to the AI Tutor. This is your go-to helper for explanations, hints, and practice questions whenever you need support.

## Recent Activity Feed

At the bottom of your dashboard, a feed shows your latest actions:

- Assignments submitted
- Practice sessions completed
- Classes joined
- Achievements earned

This feed helps you track what you have done recently and stay motivated.
